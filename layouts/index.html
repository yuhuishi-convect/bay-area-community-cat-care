    A QUICK GUIDE

    Three steps.
    A lasting difference.

    TNR helps outdoor cats live healthier lives while preventing future litters. A tipped ear is the universal sign a cat has already been fixed.

    Learn about TNR ↗

    Most clinics need an appointment. Ask about humane traps, drop-off times, fees, and recovery.
